# Formative reader study: pending

This protocol supports SP-072 / SV-007. No representative-reader results have been collected. Agent review, automated checks and a demonstration by the author do not establish reader acceptance.

Recruit technically minded readers who understand basic systems engineering but do not know the example projects. Record prior SysML and graph-tool experience. Agree the sample and acceptance thresholds with the owner before interpreting the results as acceptance evidence.

Use an immutable review snapshot, record its digest, and avoid coaching during the tasks. Ask readers to explain their interpretation in their own words:

1. Find the portability requirement and the part claiming to satisfy it. Identify the direction and meaning of the relationship.
2. Determine whether the flashlight mass has been physically verified. Locate the evidence or its absence; distinguish the inconclusive case from a failed test.
3. Follow the Blue Dog fault tree to a shared cause. Explain whether the repeated paths mean independent causes and what AND and OR require.
4. Explain which actions can proceed concurrently and what the join requires. State whether the diagram demonstrates an actual execution.
5. Explore an adjacent library element, identify the project boundary and any export limitation, then return to the exact story position.

For each task record answer accuracy with rationale, elapsed time, navigation errors, assistance, confidence and preference. Keep raw observations alongside anonymized summaries and the task rubric. Separate interface failures from missing model evidence. End with a short interview about confusing terms, excess context and useful details.

Do not turn preference alone into a comprehension claim. Revise and repeat affected tasks after material interaction changes. Report who participated and the limits of generalization. Human study scheduling and threshold agreement remain outstanding.
